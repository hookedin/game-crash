How to play

Set a stake and, if you like, an auto escape from 1.01× to 100×, then join while the next flight boards. A flight leaves every few seconds, with or without a crew, and everyone aboard rides the same rocket. In flight, press Escape or Space to take your stake times the multiplier. If the rocket crashes first, your stake is lost.

Auto escape runs in the room even if you close the page, and only a target below the crash point escapes. A flight can crash at 1.00×; at 100× everyone still aboard escapes. An escape counts when the room receives it, so network delay matters. A seat the room does not accept, such as a late one, is refunded.

A seat is a bet with the game’s developer, who pays escapes from their own bank. Each flight’s ID is the hash of a secret fixed before boarding; tap a recent crash point to check the revealed secret. That proves the crash point, not that the secret was picked fairly, the timing of escapes, or that the developer can pay.
